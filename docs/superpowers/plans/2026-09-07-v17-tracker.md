golden holds them still with a written `config.toml` and v17e should

match the header (v17g lifts this by recording at the `Inputs` level);
nothing on the advise path writes under `data/history/` or `models/`, and
the third golden test re-hashes after every run to keep it that way; the
expected advice carries the shipped chip table's quirk of a Bench Boost row
passing θ in every week of the horizon, recorded as is. Left open: the
`run_golden` hush swaps `live`'s whole `time` name for a namespace with
only `sleep`, so a future `time.monotonic()` in `data/live.py` would break
